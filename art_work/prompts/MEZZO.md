# Mezzo Prompt

Add this after `COMMON.md` for a Mezzo card.

```text
Visual class: Mezzo builder.

Create a purposeful, forward-moving avatar. Use a clear three-quarter or side-profile pose that suggests steady construction and directed progress. Keep the species silhouette strong, but make the framing slightly narrower and more active than a Macro. Place one forest-green trail marker beside the avatar at restrained but clearly visible opacity: a short pole with a broad, clearly readable flag. It must remain recognisable at 96 pixels wide. Do not use a tall pole.
```

Mezzo avatars resemble other Mezzo avatars through their visual role and green accent, not through inherited animal anatomy from a Macro card.
