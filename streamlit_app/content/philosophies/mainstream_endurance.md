# Mainstream Endurance

## Overview

Mainstream Endurance is the broad exercise-science and coaching-education model commonly taught in sport science, exercise physiology, strength and conditioning, and athletics coaching. It is not a branded coaching system; it represents the general evidence-informed training logic behind many sensible endurance plans.

## Main Emphases

- Build consistent aerobic capacity through repeatable training.
- Progress load gradually using volume, frequency, duration, intensity, terrain, and strength load as variables.
- Keep recovery central because adaptation depends on stress followed by restoration.
- Use harder running with a clear purpose and enough space around it.
- Increase specificity as the goal approaches while preserving general capacity.
- Use periodisation, tapering, and monitoring as decision tools.
- Add strength, mobility, coordination, and neuromotor work when they support running.
