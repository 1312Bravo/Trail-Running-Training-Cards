# Common

## Shared foundation

This is the shared coaching baseline for every card: durable development, athlete-centred decisions, practical trail and mountain context, clear training intent, recovery awareness, and honest professional scope.

## Main Emphases

- Build consistency, confidence, skill, resilience, and repeatable capacity over time.
- Preserve training purpose without ignoring fatigue, health, life stress, or recovery.
- Treat runners as whole people with different goals, histories, resources, and constraints.
- Use trail-specific guidance when terrain, climbing, descending, hiking, technicality, or conditions change the coaching decision.
- Prefer effort, movement quality, time on feet, and recovery response over pace when terrain makes pace misleading.
- Explain the purpose, adjustment options, and caution signals so runners can make better decisions.

## In this card library

Use this profile when a card follows the shared foundation rather than a named coaching philosophy.
