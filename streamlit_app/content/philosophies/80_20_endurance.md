# 80/20 Endurance

[Visit the 80/20 Endurance website](https://www.8020endurance.com/)

## Overview

80/20 Endurance is a structured intensity-distribution approach. Most training is deliberately low intensity, while moderate and high intensity are used purposefully, with clear workout structure and adequate recovery.

## Main Emphases

- Keep the overall training pattern predominantly low intensity.
- Protect easy work from accidental moderate effort.
- Use moderate or high intensity only when it has a clear purpose and recovery space.
- Individualise zones and select the metric that remains useful on the actual terrain.
- Progress load through manageable cycles and adapt the calendar to durability.
- Treat load data as context rather than proof of specificity or readiness.

## In this card library
