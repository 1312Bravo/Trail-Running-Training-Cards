# Lydiard Sources

## Scope

This record supports the initial interpretation in `philosophy.md`. It does not reproduce Lydiard material or represent the Lydiard Foundation officially. Most linked material was last reviewed on 2026-08-17.

## Official Lydiard Foundation Material

- **Lydiard Foundation, "The Lydiard Method."** Supports the five stated principles: aerobic capacity, feeling-based activity, response-regulated workouts, sequential development, and correct timing. [Read](https://www.lydiardfoundation.org/)
- **Lorraine Moller, "Lydiard Training Principles."** Supports aerobic conditioning, the sequential training pyramid, the named phases, and working backward from the goal. [Read](https://www.lydiardfoundation.org/about-us)
- **Lydiard Foundation, "Frequently Asked Questions."** Supports the view that the method includes all paces sequentially and adapts to available time and training frequency. [Read](https://www.lydiardfoundation.org/faq)
- **Lydiard Foundation, "Stepping Out: A Free Starter Plan."** Supports gradual progression, response-regulated recovery, and time-on-feet principles for beginners. [Read](https://www.lydiardfoundation.org/free-starter-plans)
