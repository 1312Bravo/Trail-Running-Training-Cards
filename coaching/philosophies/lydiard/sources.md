# Lydiard Sources

## Scope

This record supports our interpretation in `philosophy.md`. It does not reproduce Lydiard material or represent the Lydiard Foundation officially. Public Foundation material was reviewed on 2026-08-17; primary books and detailed course content still need page-level notes.

## Reviewed Official Lydiard Foundation Material

- **Lydiard Foundation, "The Lydiard Method."** Supports the five stated principles: aerobic capacity, feeling-based activity, response-regulated recovery, sequential development, and correct timing. [Read](https://www.lydiardfoundation.org/)
- **Lorraine Moller, "Lydiard Training Principles."** Supports the aerobic base, the inner-coach concept, recovery response, the six named phases, and working backward from the goal. [Read](https://www.lydiardfoundation.org/about-us)
- **Lydiard Foundation, "Frequently Asked Questions."** Supports the view that the method includes all paces sequentially and adapts to available time and training frequency. [Read](https://www.lydiardfoundation.org/faq)
- **Lydiard Foundation, "Stepping Out: A Free Starter Plan."** Supports gradual progression, response-regulated recovery, and time-on-feet principles for beginners. [Read](https://www.lydiardfoundation.org/free-starter-plans)

## Interpretation Boundaries

- This profile records a general running philosophy. Trail and mountain application is our documented adaptation, not a claim about the original system.
- Exact phase lengths, mileage, and historical workouts must not be transferred to cards without source review and athlete context.
- The enduring planning logic should not be confused with a requirement to copy a historical schedule.
