# Evoke Endurance Sources

## Scope

This record supports our interpretation in `philosophy.md`. It does not reproduce the source material or represent Evoke Endurance officially. Most linked material was last reviewed on 2026-08-17.

## Primary Books

- **Scott Johnston and Steve House, _Training for the New Alpinism_.** Primary background for the mountain-endurance framework; add page-level notes before relying on precise prescriptions.
- **Steve House, Scott Johnston, and Kilian Jornet, _Training for the Uphill Athlete_.** Primary source for the shared Uphill Athlete and Evoke lineage; add page-level notes before relying on precise prescriptions.

## Official Evoke Material

- **Scott Johnston, "Muscular Endurance: All You Need to Know."** Supports the aerobic-base-plus-muscular-endurance framework, caution about maintaining aerobic volume, and the role of special muscular-endurance blocks. [Read](https://evokeendurance.com/resources/muscular-endurance-all-you-need-to-know/)
- **Scott Johnston, "Capacity vs Utilization Training: A Deep Dive."** Supports the distinction between fundamental capacity work and later event-like utilisation work. [Read](https://evokeendurance.com/resources/capacity-vs-utilization-training/)
- **Scott Johnston, "How Training Works."** Supports the training-stress, recovery, and adaptation model. [Read](https://evokeendurance.com/resources/how-training-works/)
- **Scott Johnston, "Endurance: Its Evolution, Psychology, Meaning, Physiology and How to Improve Yours."** Supports the priority given to aerobic capacity and patient lower-intensity development. [Read](https://evokeendurance.com/resources/endurance-its-evolution-psychology-meaning-physiology-and-how-to-improve-yours/)
- **Evoke Endurance, "Training for Mountaineering."** Supports the sequencing of aerobic capacity, strength, muscular endurance, and more specific work. [Read](https://evokeendurance.com/resources/training-for-mountaineering/)

## Interpretation Notes

- Exact thresholds, zone definitions, load prescriptions, and muscular-endurance protocols remain context-specific.
- The profile treats Uphill Athlete as shared lineage and source material, not a second card philosophy profile.
