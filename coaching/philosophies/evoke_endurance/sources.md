# Evoke Endurance Sources

## Scope

This record supports our interpretation in `philosophy.md`. It does not reproduce source material or represent Evoke Endurance officially. Public web sources were reviewed on 2026-08-17; the primary books still need page-level notes before we use precise prescriptions.

## Primary Books To Review

- **Scott Johnston and Steve House, _Training for the New Alpinism_.** Foundational mountain-endurance background; add page-level notes before relying on detailed prescriptions.
- **Steve House, Scott Johnston, and Kilian Jornet, _Training for the Uphill Athlete_.** Shared Uphill Athlete and Evoke lineage; review running-specific sections before card authoring.

## Reviewed Official Evoke Material

- **Scott Johnston, "Muscular Endurance: All You Need to Know."** Supports the aerobic-base-plus-muscular-endurance framework, strength reserve, local muscular demand, preserving aerobic volume, and the distinction between a useful block and an overemphasised one. [Read](https://evokeendurance.com/resources/muscular-endurance-all-you-need-to-know/)
- **Scott Johnston, "Capacity vs Utilization Training: A Deep Dive."** Supports separating fundamental capacity development from later objective-like utilisation. [Read](https://evokeendurance.com/resources/capacity-vs-utilization-training/)
- **Scott Johnston, "How Training Works."** Supports the stress, recovery, and adaptation model. [Read](https://evokeendurance.com/resources/how-training-works/)
- **Scott Johnston, "Endurance: Its Evolution, Psychology, Meaning, Physiology and How to Improve Yours."** Supports the priority given to aerobic capacity and patient low-intensity development. [Read](https://evokeendurance.com/resources/endurance-its-evolution-psychology-meaning-physiology-and-how-to-improve-yours/)
- **Scott Johnston, "Updated Thinking on Aerobic Assessment for the Mountain Athlete."** Supports individual threshold assessment, heart-rate drift as one field method, and reassessment when aerobic development changes the athlete's response. [Read](https://evokeendurance.com/resources/our-latest-thinking-on-aerobic-assessment-for-the-mountain-athlete/)
- **Scott Johnston, "Zone 2: A Comprehensive Look."** Supports the threshold-anchored individual zone model and the purpose of Zone 2 aerobic work. [Read](https://evokeendurance.com/resources/zone-2-a-comprehensive-look/)
- **Scott Johnston, "Setting Your Heart Rate Zones."** Supports the need to control intensity, the role of recovery, and the limits of narrow zone precision. [Read](https://evokeendurance.com/resources/setting-your-hear-rate-zones/)
- **Evoke Endurance, "Training for Mountaineering."** Supports the ordered consideration of aerobic capacity, strength, muscular endurance, utilisation, technique, mental preparation, and tapering. [Read](https://evokeendurance.com/resources/training-for-mountaineering/)

## Interpretation Boundaries

- The books and public material cover several mountain sports; this profile identifies our running-focused interpretation rather than claiming every example transfers unchanged.
- Exact threshold relationships, tests, zone definitions, block lengths, and muscular-endurance protocols remain context-specific.
- Uphill Athlete is shared lineage and source material, not a second card philosophy profile.
