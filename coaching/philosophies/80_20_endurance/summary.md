# 80/20 Endurance Summary

## Status

**In development.** This is our documented interpretation of 80/20 Endurance material associated with Matt Fitzgerald. It is not an official 80/20 Endurance document and does not yet make `80_20_endurance` active for card authoring.

## In Brief

80/20 Endurance is a structured intensity-distribution system that keeps most training low intensity and assigns the remaining work to clearly defined moderate or high-intensity targets. It uses personalised zones and multiple intensity metrics to help runners execute the intended work instead of repeatedly drifting into an unplanned moderate-intensity rut.

## Main Emphases

- Keep the overall training distribution predominantly low intensity.
- Use individualised zones and choose the metric that works for the session.
- Define workout duration, intensity, and structure clearly.
- Treat exact execution as useful but not more important than athlete judgement.
- Add course, terrain, and environmental specificity when it is relevant to the goal.

## What It Does Not Mean

- It does not mean that every week must mathematically equal one fixed ratio.
- It does not mean moderate work never has a role.
- It does not mean watch zones should overrule terrain, conditions, or recovery.
