# 80/20 Endurance Sources

## Scope

This record supports our interpretation in `philosophy.md`. It does not reproduce 80/20 Endurance material or represent the system officially. Public web sources were reviewed on 2026-08-17; the primary book and detailed plan material still need page-level review.

## Primary Books To Review

- **Matt Fitzgerald, _80/20 Running_.** Review the applicable edition and passages before creating cards with precise ratio, zone, progression, or workout claims.

## Reviewed Official 80/20 Material

- **Matt Fitzgerald, "Intensity Guidelines for 80/20 Running."** Supports personalised zones and the use of pace, heart rate, power, and perceived effort. [Read](https://www.8020endurance.com/intensity-guidelines-for-8020-running/)
- **Matt Fitzgerald, "Understanding Your 80/20 Run Plan."** Supports structured workouts, deliberate intensity balance, athlete judgement, and avoiding untargeted moderate effort. [Read](https://www.8020endurance.com/understanding-your-8020-run-plan/)
- **80/20 Endurance, "How to Adjust Your 80/20 Training Plan for a Specific Course or Conditions."** Supports course, terrain, and environmental specificity. [Read](https://www.8020endurance.com/adjusting-your-8020-training-plan/)
- **80/20 Endurance, "Developing Endurance Fitness: Science Limits on How to Train."** Supports a low-intensity majority, gradual volume growth, recovery weeks, and the distinction between research findings and applied long-term practice. [Read](https://www.8020endurance.com/on-the-limits-of-science-as-a-guide-on-how-to-train/)
- **80/20 Endurance, "Your Performance Management Chart Is Lying To You."** Supports treating load scores as incomplete without intensity composition and event specificity. [Read](https://www.8020endurance.com/performance-management-chart-is-lying-to-you/)
- **80/20 Endurance, "Understanding Your 80/20 Endurance Master Run Training Plan."** Supports adapting microcycle length, cross-training, and recovery density for less durable or slower-recovering runners. [Read](https://www.8020endurance.com/understanding-your-80-20-endurance-master-run-training-plan/)
- **80/20 Endurance, "Frequency of Hard and Easy Workouts."** Supports viewing the distribution at a larger scale and protecting recovery between demanding work. [Read](https://www.8020endurance.com/frequency-of-hard-and-easy-workouts/)

## Interpretation Boundaries

- Do not turn the brand name into a rigid per-session arithmetic rule.
- Exact zones, testing protocols, workout codes, and phase lengths require primary-material review and athlete context.
- Trail adaptation in this profile is our running-focused application of the system's specificity principles.
