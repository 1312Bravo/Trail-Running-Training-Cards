## Official 80/20 Material

- **Matt Fitzgerald, "Intensity Guidelines for 80/20 Running."** Supports the personalised zone system and the use of pace, heart rate, and power. [Read](https://www.8020endurance.com/intensity-guidelines-for-8020-running/)
- **Matt Fitzgerald, "Understanding Your 80/20 Run Plan."** Supports the duration, intensity, and structure model; the deliberate treatment of untargeted moderate zones; and the role of athlete judgement. [Read](https://www.8020endurance.com/understanding-your-8020-run-plan/)
- **80/20 Endurance, "How to Adjust Your 80/20 Training Plan for a Specific Course or Conditions."** Supports terrain and condition specificity for trail and ultramarathon goals. [Read](https://www.8020endurance.com/adjusting-your-8020-training-plan/)
