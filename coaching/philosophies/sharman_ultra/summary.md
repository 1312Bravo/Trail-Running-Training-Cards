# Sharman Ultra Summary

## Status

**In development.** This is our documented interpretation of Sharman Ultra Coaching, associated with Ian Sharman. It is not official Sharman Ultra material and does not yet make `sharman_ultra` active for card authoring.

## In Brief

Sharman Ultra is an adaptive ultrarunning coaching system: understand the athlete and their life, use established endurance knowledge and practical ultra experience, teach the runner to interpret their response, and prepare the capabilities and execution skills the real event requires. Its key value is applied coaching judgement rather than one publicly defined proprietary workout model.

## Main Emphases

- Build rapport and adapt the plan to goals, history, lifestyle, and available recovery.
- Give every session a clear purpose and maximise its useful benefit.
- Educate the runner to make better training and racing adjustments.
- Integrate broad endurance knowledge with practical ultrarunning experience.
- Prepare actual course demands and race execution in proportion to their relevance.
- Offer accessible substitutions while naming the specificity they cannot reproduce.

## What It Does Not Mean

- It does not make every preference a sufficient reason to change the plan.
- It does not make coach experience a substitute for evidence or transparent reasoning.
- It does not yet provide enough public detail for exact Sharman-specific session protocols.
