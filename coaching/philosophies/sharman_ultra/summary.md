# Sharman Ultra Summary

## Status

**In development.** This is our documented interpretation of Sharman Ultra Coaching, associated with Ian Sharman. It is not an official Sharman Ultra document and does not yet make `sharman_ultra` active for card authoring.

## In Brief

Sharman Ultra presents a shared ultrarunning methodology adapted to the athlete's life, goals, and response rather than a cookie-cutter schedule. It explicitly combines influences from Tim Noakes, Arthur Lydiard, Pete Pfitzinger, NASM, USATF, and practical ultrarunning experience.

Its distinctive contribution is not a single proprietary intensity model. It is an athlete-specific, experience-led interpretation of established endurance principles for diverse ultrarunning goals.

## Main Emphases

- Build a coach-athlete understanding before prescribing training.
- Adapt established endurance concepts for the goal, lifestyle, and response of the runner.
- Teach runners to understand their body and make better training and racing adjustments.
- Use practical ultrarunning experience alongside formal coaching knowledge.
- Keep coaching open to new findings and challenge conventional wisdom.

## What It Does Not Mean

- It does not mean every personal preference justifies a different plan.
- It does not mean practical experience alone replaces evidence or structured reasoning.
- It does not provide enough public detail yet for exact Sharman-specific session protocols.
