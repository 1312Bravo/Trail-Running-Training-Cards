# Sharman Ultra Sources

## Scope

This record supports our interpretation in `philosophy.md`. It does not reproduce Sharman Ultra material or represent the team officially. Public web sources were reviewed on 2026-08-17. The profile is deliberately cautious because its public material explains coaching stance and influences more fully than a detailed proprietary training model.

- **Sharman Ultra, "Coaching."** Supports the rapport-first and non-cookie-cutter stance; lifestyle-fit planning; maximising the benefit of training sessions; named methodological influences; athlete education; practical guidance; and openness to learning. [Read](https://www.sharmanultra.com/coaching)
- **Sharman Ultra, "Ian Sharman."** Supports the role of long practical ultrarunning experience, formal coaching background, and broad event experience in the team's method. [Read](https://www.sharmanultra.com/coaches/iansharman)
- **Sharman Ultra, "Podcasts/Media."** Supports the existence of public material on general training principles, power-hiking, altitude, ageing athletes, advice quality, and rediscovering passion for ultrarunning. [Read](https://www.sharmanultra.com/podcasts-media)

## Interpretation Boundaries

- Do not attribute the complete Noakes, Lydiard, or Pfitzinger systems to Sharman Ultra without direct evidence.
- Practical ultrarunning experience is a useful source of context, not proof that one strategy suits every athlete.
- Exact Sharman Ultra session protocols and progression rules remain out of scope until directly reviewed.
