# Macro Prompt

Add this after `COMMON.md` for a Macro card.

```text
Visual class: Macro master.

Create the strongest and most grounded avatar in the system. Use a broad, calm head-and-shoulders or compact full-body pose with a decisive species silhouette. It should feel stable, capable, and long-range rather than hurried or aggressive. Give the primary contour its strongest line weight. It must remain recognisable at 96 pixels wide. Do not include the Macro mountain symbol; the app renders that separately.
```

Macro avatars are masters of the Macro visual class. They establish the quality bar for clarity, maturity, and composure; they do not need literal visual descendants.
