# Micro Prompt

Add this after `COMMON.md` for a Micro card.

```text
Visual class: Micro organizer.

Create a compact, alert avatar with a balanced, ready pose. It should suggest control, adaptability, and a short planning horizon rather than broad authority or immediate action. Keep the silhouette readable in a small footprint. It must remain recognisable at 96 pixels wide. Do not include the Micro compass symbol; the app renders that separately.
```

Micro avatars resemble other Micro avatars through compact composition, alertness, and the violet accent. They do not inherit their species or silhouette from linked Mezzo cards.
