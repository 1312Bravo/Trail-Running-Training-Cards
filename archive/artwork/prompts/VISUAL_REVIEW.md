# Avatar Visual Review

Run this review before accepting an avatar set. Review avatars at their intended white-card size, around 96 pixels wide, with file names and card titles hidden.

## Species Test

- Can a reviewer name the intended animal immediately?
- Does the species read from its full-body outer silhouette and 2-4 key features, rather than from a title or many internal lines?
- Does the avatar still read if viewed quickly, from farther away, or next to three other cards?

## Realism Test

- Does the animal have believable anatomy: head shape, neck/shoulder connection, eye/beak/muzzle/ear/horn placement?
- Does it avoid abstract loop shapes that only make sense after explanation?
- Is the full-body pose clear at small size? If not, simplify the pose before cropping to a bust/profile.
- Are pale fills and detail lines helping recognition rather than making the image busy?

## Unique-Silhouette Test

- Compare avatars side by side with all coloured accents hidden.
- Does each avatar have a genuinely different body proportion, tail/wing/leg shape, muzzle/beak, ear or horn placement, and pose?
- Would any pair still look like the same generic base if their species details were removed? If yes, redraw the primary contour before adding detail.
- Does the set avoid looking like one template with swapped accessories? If not, stop before replacing more files.

## Planning-Level Colour Test

- Can a reviewer identify the planning level from its colour signature without reading the title?
- Is the blue Macro mountain, green Mezzo trail marker, violet Micro compass, or amber Session stopwatch clearly visible at 96 pixels wide?
- Does the colour clarify the role without taking over the avatar?

## Personal-Mark Test

- Is the personal mark a quiet soft-gray detail on the animal, rather than a competing badge?
- Does it remain secondary to both the animal and the coloured planning-level symbol?

## Mature-Style Test

- Does the avatar feel like a calm field-guide engraving rather than a mascot, logo, or cartoon?
- Are the lines purposeful, smooth, and free of unnecessary crossings or loops?
